Bill of materials

What gets bought, what gets welded and cut in the shop, and what needs a machine shop — by system, with the candidate part where one has been picked and a rough cost where one has been estimated. Prices are 2026 list or marketplace figures from the notebook and are ranges on purpose.

Priced so far: four corners with controllers, air and brakes ~$4,000–6,500 (before rims, tires and machining), the battery sled ~$3,600 DIY, six pods ~$5,300 in materials (the driver pod ~$200 more) — about $13,000–15,500. Not yet priced: frame steel and deck, rims and tires, the steering rack and column, the roof (tube, fascia, panels), the lighting controller and harness, machining, stairs and ottomans, the charger, and the transport cart and dollies.
