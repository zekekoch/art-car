What changes mechanically?

Retain: matched motor / GT transaxle, front axle and steering gear, leaf springs, service and parking brakes.

Fabricate: pod carrier, receivers, stairs, guards and roof; engineer the forward driver-control connections.

Investigate first: installed gear ratio and axle version. A deeper reduction can improve crawl torque. Controller current does not increase bearing or axle load capacity.

Transport is a separate limit

Trailer view shows the rolling base only. Removable art must be loaded separately and secured. Ramp figures exclude operator; verify actual weights, load distribution and rental loading limits. A complete packing rack and tow vehicle are unresolved. In transplant mode, the base mass is an unweighed parts allowance; the factory vehicle payload rating does not transfer to a new frame.

Three measurements unlock the next revision

1. Complete data plate, serial number and axle / motor / controller labels.
2. Four tire contact locations and empty axle scale weights with batteries.
3. Bare structural mounting points and battery/service-lid envelopes.
