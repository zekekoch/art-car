# Drivetrain review — 12 September 2026

The four independent hub motors can be bought, but the proposed suspension and steering corners are custom mechanisms. Expect welding and fixture work plus a small, consequential package of outsourced machining. The motor's axle interface, torque reaction, steering pivots and brake alignment are the hard parts. A motor is not a complete rated automotive wheel end.

Reviewed README.md, loveseat.md, all four existing HTML studies and the laser-model parameter source. Existing files include uncommitted design work; this review and the new model are additive. The later frame model (66 in wheelbase, 43 in track, 24 in deck) is the working baseline; the notebook and laser model contain earlier geometry.

## Errors and unresolved assumptions

1. **Motor and tire interfaces are mixed.** The notebook says QS205 12 in / 3 kW, while the frame draws a single-sided shaft, 10 in hub and 20×10-10 tire. A 20×10-10 tire requires a 10 in rim. The QS205 **car** motor is a separate 3 kW motor for a 12 in or larger wheel. Its stationary shaft needs a positively located mount and torque reaction; the wheel flange rotates. It does not simply bolt to a generic trailer spindle. The scooter and car variants are different parts. [QS car motor](https://www.qsmotor.com/product/3000w-205-car-motor/), [QS scooter variant](https://www.cnqsmotor.com/product/10-inch-single-shaft-hub-motor-qs205-3000-watt-v3-72v-motor/).

2. **Published QS information does not close the design.** The car listing advertises 48–96 V in one section and 72–96 V in another. Its linked image is named “Drawing of 4000W Car Motor” and shows 4×114.3 mm, while the text specifies 4×100 mm. The other QS listing specifies 4×100, a 60 mm center bore, M24×1.5 shaft thread and 16 kg mass; IP ratings and peak torque also differ between pages. The new model uses the text's flange interface, with a 222 mm housing / 68 mm central barrel / 221.5 mm overall axial reference envelope from that legacy drawing, explicitly provisional. Obtain the exact ordered variant's drawing before machining or ordering a rim. None of the pages reviewed establishes allowable radial load, side load or overturning moment at this car's offset. [QS variant](https://www.qsmotor.cc/QS205-Single-Shaft-E-Car-Hub-Motor50H-3000W-V3-Type-p.html), [legacy drawing](https://www.qsmotor.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Drawing-of-4000W-Car-Motor.jpg).

3. **Low-speed torque is not established by 3 kW or peak torque.** For a 2,000 kg vehicle on 20 in tires at 5 mph, wheel speed is 84 rpm. At assumed rolling-resistance coefficients of 0.03 / 0.06 / 0.10, equal four-wheel torque is 37 / 75 / 125 N·m per wheel. A 2% grade adds 25 N·m, and 0.15 m/s² acceleration adds 19 N·m. These are scenarios, not measurements of playa resistance. A 22.49 in tire increases these torques by 12.5%. The quoted 40 N·m is plausible for firm ground only. At 40 N·m and 84 rpm each wheel makes just 352 W mechanically; copper losses, phase current and cooling determine whether it can do this continuously. Request continuous torque and temperature data at approximately 75–85 rpm, plus starting/stall time limits, for the actual winding and controller. Do not divide 3 kW by low rpm and call the result continuous torque.

4. **The steering animation is not a linkage solution.** `frame-3d.html` assigns both front knuckles the same angle and draws rods to suit; their lengths change. It prints ideal Ackermann angles but does not use them. The leading steering arms point inward despite a rack ahead of the axle, so the linkage also needs an Ackermann geometry review. Its default vertical kingpin is 10 in inboard of the tire center: large scrub radius and steering/shaft bending loads. With 1,000 lb on a corner that offset alone is 10,000 in·lb at the kingpin. Steering lock must be checked against rails, spring, arm, receiver and fender through bump and droop, including the changing wheel-center position. The new model solves fixed-length tie rods for a **proposed rear-of-axle rack**, and reports closure failures and a limited tire/rail/receiver interference screen. This is still not a validated suspension.

5. **Eight inches of kneel is not verified.** The old readout checks tire/seat and sled clearance without articulating arms, checking dampers, or enforcing spring limits. The D2500 is at most 7 in diameter (not the default 6), 2.8 in compressed, 10.5 in extended, with 5.5–7.5 in design height. With a 16 in arm and spring 10 in from the pivot, spring motion ratio is 0.625: spring force is approximately 1.6× sprung wheel load. Air-spring stroke is not wheel travel. The new model's nominal 6.5 in spring height allows only 5.9 in wheel rise by that simplified height calculation; its larger battery enclosure reaches 3 in ground clearance after 5.5 in drop. Steering may bind first. Separate dampers, bump stops, droop limits and mechanical supports for service are missing in the original geometry. Spring mounting-angle limits remain to be checked. [D2500 58343](https://www.airliftperformance.com/product/58343).

6. **Corner and gross weights are undercounted.** The existing sum includes tube, pack, pods, roof and stairs, but omits motor/wheel assemblies, bearings, knuckles, brakes, springs, dampers, controllers, much of the plate and wiring, and deck material. Four 16 kg car motors alone add 141 lb, before tires and rims. Twelve 200 lb riders add 2,400 lb before any car: 600 lb/corner therefore cannot be the loaded design case. Also use the driver's approximately 185 lb pod, not six identical 120 lb placeholders. Create a weighed BOM, individual corner reactions and asymmetric passenger cases before rating the running gear. If “1/2 in deck plate” means a full steel plate, 54×120×0.5 in is about 919 lb before cutouts; specify its material and coverage.

7. **Pivot readouts are moment-couple estimates, not bearing ratings.** `(side force × tire radius + vertical force × offset)/bearing spacing` is one moment contribution. It omits the direct reactions, spring loads, braking loads, combined load directions, shaft stress, bearing life, housing/weld stiffness and seals. Arbitrary green thresholds cannot validate a bearing. Similarly air pressure alone is not wheel load: effective area depends on height, motion ratio matters, and friction/dynamic loads matter. A tipping warning needs calibrated load/height information and a vehicle dynamics model.

8. **Regen plus a parking lever leaves a service-brake gap.** Add a foot-operated friction brake that remains available with a full pack, tripped BMS or disabled controllers, plus a mechanically held parking brake. The new model reserves a tandem-master pedal assembly, four service calipers and two rear mechanical parking calipers. These are layout envelopes pending a brake torque, pedal ratio, balance and thermal calculation. Do not assume an arbitrary caliper fits a QS rotor: diameter, thickness, mounting PCD, offset and pad sweep must all match. Corner removal also involves a brake hose, a damper, torque mount, and front tie rod (plus rear parking cable), beyond the claimed two connectors. [Wilwood mechanical example](https://www.wilwood.com/Calipers/CaliperProd?itemno=120-1360).

9. **Battery capacity and box dimensions are optimistic.** 32×3.2 V×280 Ah = 28.672 kWh nominal; a chosen 80–90% operating window gives 22.9–25.8 kWh before conversion losses. Two 16S strings are 51.2 V nominal and up to 58.4 V if charged to 3.65 V/cell. A raw 16×2 cell arrangement, using 72×173.7×207.5 mm cells, is 45.35×13.68×8.17 in. A 46×14×9 in *finished box* scarcely allows walls, insulation, compression hardware, busbars, covers, terminals or service access. The new model reserves 50×21×10.5 in, including an electrical bay; this is a packaging allowance, not an enclosure design. EVE has multiple revisions: 0.5C test conditions alone do not establish every revision's maximum current. Confirm the purchased cell specification. Parallel packs require separately rated protection, controlled connection, current sharing and defined behavior when one drops out; “limp home” is not automatic. [EVE North America LF280K sheet](https://www.evebatteryusa.com/_files/ugd/abe55e_7ba2e98dd2884f8d978b861be2b471e2.pdf).

10. **The energy budget has an arithmetic error.** 0.3–1 kW lighting ×8 h = 2.4–8 kWh, not 1–3. Drive 2–4 kW ×3 h = 6–12 kWh. Subtracting an assumed 4 kWh of solar gives 4.4–16 kWh/day before other loads, losses or reserve. Solar energy depends on actual conditions, cleaning and shading. The blanket every-other-day charging promise is not supported by the full range.

11. **The transport plan cannot discard the tow limit.** The stated U-Haul dimensions, 2,290 lb empty weight, 3,710 lb cargo and 1,810 lb ramp ratings match U-Haul. Cruise America's FAQ still requires towing weight within 2,500 lb. That leaves only 210 lb above this empty trailer, so that combination cannot carry the proposed car within those stated restrictions. Prior towing does not change the limit. Resolve the tow vehicle/trailer combination separately. [U-Haul](https://www.uhaul.com/Trailers/6x12-Utility-Trailer-With-Ramp-Rental/HO/), [Cruise America](https://www-prod.cruiseamerica.com/rv-rentals/renters-resources/rv-rental-faqs).

12. **Event rules and guards need correction.** BRC's vehicle speed limit is 5 mph or less, including daytime. Its current mutant-vehicle criteria also call for sturdy guards generally 36–48 in above the floor, with stated ingress/egress allowances. The 25 in rim and 12 in driver gate merit a DMV design discussion; the current notes do not establish acceptance. [Driving rules](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/bring-your-mutant-vehicle/vehicle-rules-and-protocols/), [vehicle criteria](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/bring-your-mutant-vehicle/mv-criteria-and-licensing/).

13. **Pod receivers have both stale notes and a load-reference problem.** Text alternates 14/26 in spacing, 2×3/2×2 arms and 8/12 in U legs. The current single-pod stress code measures rider and pod lever arms from the pod center without adding the inset distance to the supporting chassis rail. With the rail moved inward approximately 12 in, that omission can materially understate the moment at the rail (roughly an extra 520×12 = 6,240 in·lb static for a 520 lb pod+riders before dynamic factors). A proper free-body diagram must locate sleeve contact reactions and rail/crossmember supports; a commercial complete hitch's rating cannot be transferred to two raw receiver tubes. The claim that receivers mean “nothing fatigues loose” is incorrect. Pin holes, bearing contact, welds, retention and anti-rattle provisions still need checking. This model keeps only chassis sleeves; no pods or roof are drawn.

## What you buy, fabricate and machine

| Assembly | Buy | Weld / cut / drill | Precision work / remaining decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motor + wheel | 4 QS205 car motors, candidate 12 in rims and rated turf tires | Guards, connector supports | Vendor-confirm motor load rating and exact shaft/flange; order matching rated wheels, do not re-drill wheels casually |
| Rear carrier | Bearings/bushings, fasteners | Arm, gussets, module mounting plates | Shaft clamp/torque block, coaxial pivot sleeves and brake offsets |
| Front carrier | Bushings/bearings, rod ends, shaft U-joints | Arm, tower, mounts | Kingpin/bearing seats, thrust faces, antirotation mount, knuckle and steering-arm interfaces; likely outsource |
| Steering | Suitable rack, column, sealed joints | Rack cradle and column supports | Rack width/travel and rods are not selected; solve geometry first; final toe alignment and bump-steer measurement |
| Suspension | D2500 ×4, dampers, stops, compressor/manifold | Spring and damper towers, pivot hangers | Coaxial pivots; verify spring angles, forces and damper stroke before buying |
| Brakes | Matched rotors/calipers, tandem master, pedal and parking mechanism | Hose tabs, brackets | Rotor hats/adapters and caliper alignment if no matched kit; brake sizing and dust protection |
| Battery / electrical | Cells, qualified protection components, controllers, connectors | Removable cradle, protective enclosure, harness support | Controller tuning, BMS/regen coordination, pack protection and mechanical retention |

Buying bushings and shafts does not eliminate alignment: weldments may need line-reaming after welding. A drill press, bandsaw, welder and jig cover the frame and brackets; a machine shop can make the concentrated set of precision parts. You need not buy a mill or lathe, but someone needs to own those interfaces and tolerances.

If minimizing machining dominates, compare this with a **complete rated utility-cart front axle/steering/brake assembly and matched electric rear transaxle**. Keeping their bearings, shafts and brake interfaces intact concentrates custom work in frame mounts. A random golf-cart donor is not automatically rated for twelve people; select by gross and axle loads. Four hubs plus independent air suspension remains a materially more custom route.

## Model scope and provenance

Open `drivetrain-3d.html`. It is an engineering packaging study, not manufacturing CAD or a purchase-approved BOM. No pods, loveseats, roof or riders are drawn. It includes a frame, receiver sleeves, individual arm pivots, kingpins, torque blocks, motors, wheel fasteners, brake envelopes, air springs, dampers, steering rack/rods/column, pedals, battery cells, four controller envelopes, pneumatic tank and routed services.

- Baseline: frame-model 120×54 in envelope, 66 in wheelbase, 43 in tire-center track, 24 in deck datum, rails centered ±14 in.
- Tire candidate: Carlstar Multi-Trac C/S 574353, nominal 23×10.50-12, catalog mounted dimensions 571.2×259.3 mm (22.49×10.21 in), specified 8.5 in rim width; table lists 798.3 kg rated load, 16.1 km/h and 32 psi. Those ratings require the manufacturer's conditions and do not rate the motor, rim or complete corner. Tire deflection/contact patch is not simulated. [Carlstar table](https://www.carlstar.com/our-products/product-detail/multi-trac/).
- Motor: QS205 car 50H V3 candidate; flange 4×100 mm, CB60 and M24×1.5 per variant text. Legacy dimension envelope is mixed-revision and visibly marked provisional. Internal bearings are diagrammatic, with no inferred bearing part number or capacity.
- Air spring: D2500 58343 dimensions from manufacturer; bellows contour is illustrative.
- Controller candidate: Kelly KLS4830S; manufacturer lists 36–48 V nominal, 30–62 V operating, 120 A continuous / 300 A one-minute boost. Those are controller ratings, not demonstrated wheel torque. Body envelope in this model is an allowance, not a drilling template. Full-charge regen coordination and voltage margin need analysis. [Kelly KLS-S](https://kellycontroller.com/shop/kls-s/).
- Brakes, rack, dampers, kingpin and fastener sizes are labeled proposed/unselected; no implied catalog compatibility. Service and parking brake shapes illustrate separate functions.
- The revised 12 in tire is larger than the old tire. With 26 in receiver spacing, 2.5 in sleeves and the catalog 22.49 in diameter, nominal fore/aft clearance is only about 0.51 in per side at straight-ahead ride height. This does not include tire growth, compliance or steering sweep.
- Positive kneel lowers the sprung frame while keeping tire centers at catalog radius above ground; arms maintain 16 in center length. The front knuckle follows its arm, so caster changes. Rear arms are mirrored leading arms. This exposes a disadvantage of this layout rather than concealing it.
- Steering angles are solved separately from constant tie-rod lengths in 3D. No solution is explicitly reported. The ideal Ackermann values are shown as comparison only. Neither smooth animation nor a passed limited interference screen establishes safe travel.
- OBJ export preserves named mesh parts in **meters, Y up** for Blender/Fusion; an accompanying MTL preserves fabrication colors. Browser orbit views and exported geometry use the same scene. No .blend file is claimed.

Before freezing the chassis, the most useful next physical prototype is **one front corner and a rack fixture**, tested at intended vertical/side loads through its full travel and steer range. First obtain an exact, load-rated motor/wheel/brake package and a continuous low-speed motor test. Those decisions control the rest of the geometry.
