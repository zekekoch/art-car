Corners use single-shaft (ATV / golf-cart style) hub motors bolted to a spindle, so every arm is single-sided on a wide pivot. Fronts end in a kingpin; a rack ahead of the axle drives tie rods; the column runs under the driver pod to a wheel, with a quick-disconnect at the pod joint (red ring).
