The steering arms point forward and outward from the kingpins, 3" above the hub centre so the rods clear the arm legs; the rack sits on the frame at the ball joints' own height and station, so the transverse rods are square to the ball joints' arc and the toe change over bump is second order (the readout shows it; a centre-take-off rack with the pivots near the centreline halves it again). Each wheel's angle is solved from its fixed-length tie rod ("no solution" when a rod cannot close). The readout compares the outer wheel with ideal Ackermann for the inner, sweeps the rack to find lock against the arm legs, spring, rail and receivers, and reports the toe change over ±bump travel. 6.5" is about the least hub-height scrub this single-sided motor package allows; kingpin inclination brings the ground-level figure down.

Cloverleaf: six overlapping leaves, one over each pod, as two halves split at mid-length. Each half stands on four posts at the leaf valleys (outer pair at the frame corners, inner pair flanking the walkway, so they double as grab handles). The rim is a shallow box beam whose outboard face is the lit fascia; two 100 W flexible panels lie on each leaf. Leaves centred inboard of the pods close the hole over the aisle; slide leafX / leafZ to trade aisle cover against pod-rim cover.

A rack behind the front axle drives fixed-length tie rods; the column runs under the end rail to the driver pod with a quick-disconnect at the pod joint (red ring).
